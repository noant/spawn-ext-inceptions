# Researcher report (inc-explorer / inc-researcher)

Response template (Output format) that the `inc-explorer` / `inc-researcher` subagent fills in its final response to the parent.

```markdown
# Research report: {direction / topic}

## Direction
{The direction line that was set, or "free".}

## Research file
{Link to the research file: res/{research}.md}

## Summary
{Brief summary (2-5 sentences) — what was found.}

## Gaps
{What could not be found, what requires clarification.}

## Model
My model: {model}
```
