# Final report to the user

Response template (Output format) that the `inc-engineer` fills when showing the final report to the user.

```markdown
# Final report: {Inception {N} — {Title}}

## Outcome
{Success | Partial | Failed}

## What was achieved
{What was achieved — briefly.}

## Why (reasons for the result)
{Reasons for success or failure.}

## Highlighted problems
{Highlighted problems that surfaced.}

## Next step proposal
{Proposal to continue the cycle in the next attempt — taking into account the previous work and context of all steps and problems.}

## Extracted rules
{Extracted rules, if any.}
```
